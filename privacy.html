Privacy Policy

ZeroAI Publisher uses TikTok OAuth to connect the authorized user's TikTok account.

The app may store TikTok access tokens locally only for the purpose of uploading or publishing content after user confirmation.

ZeroAI Publisher does not sell personal data, does not share personal data with third parties, and does not provide public access to user data.

The user can disconnect TikTok access at any time.
