ZeroAI Publisher

ZeroAI Publisher is a personal automation tool used to create and publish educational AI-related content.

The app connects to TikTok through OAuth and can upload or publish videos only after user confirmation.

The app is currently used by the authorized owner of the TikTok account and does not provide public user registration.
